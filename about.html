The Mayuri Story

Born from a Reverence for Ancient Indian Ornamentation

In 1998, master goldsmith Rajeshwar Sen noticed that the priceless royal jewellery traditions of Rajasthan, Hyderabad, and South India were becoming increasingly inaccessible for modern brides to wear during multi-day festivities. Real heirloom jewellery was either too heavy for hours of dancing or too risky to carry to destination venues.

Mayuri Jewels was established with a singular vision: to recreate the regal weight, intricate stone setting, and lustrous golden glow of heritage Indian jewels using skin-safe brass alloys, Austrian crystals, and 24K micron gold electroplating.

Values & Purpose

Guiding Principles of Our Atelier

Rooted in generational craft, ethical metallurgy, and timeless Indian splendour.

Our Sacred Mission

To make every woman feel like royalty on her most memorable celebrations by delivering master-crafted imitation jewellery that celebrates Indian heritage, ensures hypoallergenic skin safety, and remains accessible without compromise.

Our Enduring Vision

To be the world’s most trusted Indian artificial jewellery atelier, sustaining vanishing indigenous handicrafts such as Cuttack silver filigree, Rajasthani Jadau, and Hyderabad lac work while embracing modern non-tarnish metallurgy.

Behind the Sparkle

The Art of Mayuri Craftsmanship

Every single piece passes through eight stages of meticulous hand-finishing before it reaches your keepsake box.

STAGE 1
Architectural Wax Carving

Our designers study museum archives and temple sculptures to hand-carve master wax silhouettes with authentic proportions.

STAGE 2
Precision Brass Forging

Cast in pure, lead-free and nickel-free jeweller's brass alloy that provides heirloom weight without skin irritation.

STAGE 3
Foil-Backed Jadau Setting

Stones are handset with pure silver/gold foil backings that bounce ambient light to mimic natural uncut diamonds.

Master artisan setting micro-crystals under magnifying loupes in our Mumbai atelier.

STAGE 4
Hand-Painted Meenakari

The reverse sides of our chokers and jhumkas are painted with vibrant mineral enamels and baked in ceramic kilns.

STAGE 5
24K Micron Gold Electroplating

Plated with heavy micron coats of genuine 24K gold and sealed with a transparent anti-tarnish protective glaze.

STAGE 6
Hand-Strung Silk Dori & Pearls

Strung with durable cotton-silk threads and micro seed pearls for a comfortable, snag-free neck contour.

Terms of Care & Service
1. Jewellery Care Instructions

Keep all imitation, Kundan, and lac jewellery away from direct contact with perfumes, sanitizers, hairsprays, and water. Wipe gently with a dry, soft cotton cloth after every wear before storing.

2. Storage Recommendation

Always store ornaments in airtight ziplock pouches or inside the complimentary Mayuri velvet box. Avoid velvet foam cases that retain atmospheric moisture.

3. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

In the unlikely event of damage during transit, record an unboxing video and report it to our customer care within 7 days of delivery for immediate, hassle-free replacement.

4. Plating & Color Longevity

Our 24K and antique micron plating is engineered for long-lasting festive wear. Natural oxidation depends on skin chemistry and humidity; proper care ensures years of radiant shine.
