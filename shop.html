Terms of Care & Service
1. Jewellery Care Instructions

Keep all imitation, Kundan, and lac jewellery away from direct contact with perfumes, sanitizers, hairsprays, and water. Wipe gently with a dry, soft cotton cloth after every wear before storing.

2. Storage Recommendation

Always store ornaments in airtight ziplock pouches or inside the complimentary Mayuri velvet box. Avoid velvet foam cases that retain atmospheric moisture.

3. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

In the unlikely event of damage during transit, record an unboxing video and report it to our customer care within 7 days of delivery for immediate, hassle-free replacement.

4. Plating & Color Longevity

Our 24K and antique micron plating is engineered for long-lasting festive wear. Natural oxidation depends on skin chemistry and humidity; proper care ensures years of radiant shine.
