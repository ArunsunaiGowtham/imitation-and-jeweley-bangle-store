Customer Trust & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is sacred to us. Learn how Mayuri Jewels & Bangles secures your personal data, protects online transactions, and respects your shopping preferences.

Official Store Policy • Effective 2026
Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect customer names, shipping addresses, phone numbers, and email IDs solely to fulfill jewellery orders, provide dispatch tracking, and send requested styling consultations. We do not gather extraneous personal data or background information without your direct consent.

2. Payment Security & 256-Bit Encryption

We do not store your debit/credit card numbers or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are processed securely through certified, encrypted 256-bit payment gateways (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). Your banking details remain confidential and encrypted at all stages.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your personal contact information is never sold, leased, rented, or distributed to any third-party marketing agencies. Contact data is shared exclusively with our verified courier delivery partners solely for delivering your jewellery parcel.

4. Cookies & Local Preferences

We use local browser storage strictly to preserve your cart items, saved wishlist pieces, and light/dark mode theme preferences across visits. We do not employ intrusive tracking cookies or unauthorized cross-site tracking scripts.

5. Customer Rights & Data Rectification

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may at any point request a summary of your stored contact info, update an address, or request permanent deletion of your profile by emailing our privacy desk at care@mayurijewels.com.

Need Further Clarification?

Our customer support team is available Monday through Saturday to answer questions regarding our privacy practices.
