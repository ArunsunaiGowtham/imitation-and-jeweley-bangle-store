Preservation Guidelines & Service Commitments

Terms of Care & Service

Handcrafted imitation jewellery requires mindful preservation. Follow our expert care recommendations to preserve 24K micron plating and gemstone sparkle for years.

Official Customer Care & Quality Guarantee
Edition: 2026 Guidelines

1. Chemical & Moisture Protection

Always apply perfumes, deodorants, hairsprays, and cosmetics before putting on your jewellery. Avoid exposing Kundan, polki, brass alloys, or lac bangles to water, pool chlorine, salt water, and household cleaning chemicals.

2. Storage Recommendations

Store each jewellery item separately in individual airtight ziplock pouches or within the velvet-lined jewellery cases provided with your Mayuri purchase. Never use open spongy foam boxes that trap atmospheric moisture and induce oxidation.

3. Cleaning & After-Wear Protocol

After each celebration or wear, gently wipe down the metallic and gemstone surfaces with a dry, clean microfibre or soft cotton cloth to remove perspiration and skin oils. Never use liquid jewellery cleaners, abrasive powders, or ultrasonic baths.

4. 7-Day Transit Replacement Guarantee

Every parcel is bubble-wrapped with multi-layer tamper-evident seals. In the rare case of transit damage or missing pieces, simply record an unboxing video and notify our WhatsApp helpline within 7 days of package arrival for immediate replacement.

5. Micron Plating & Wear Longevity

Our 24K antique and gold micron coatings are formulated for long-lasting festive wear. While natural tone mellowing depends on personal skin pH levels and storage humidity, mindful care according to these guidelines ensures radiant shine for years.
